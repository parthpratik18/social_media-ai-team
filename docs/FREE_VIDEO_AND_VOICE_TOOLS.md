# Free AI video creation and voice-over toolkit

**Purpose:** practical ways to make short-form videos at ₹0 using a combination of free tiers, trials, browser tools, mobile apps and open-source software. This is not a promise of unlimited free generation. Tool access, free credits, watermarking, export quality, country availability and licensing change often. Verify the tool's current plan and terms on its official website before using outputs commercially.

**Checked:** 21 November 2026. This guide is a starting shortlist, not a real-time availability guarantee.

## 2) Free AI voice generation / text-to-speech

| Tool | Best use | Free-plan caveat | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| TTSMaker | Downloadable narration for short videos | Check current character limits, attribution requirements and commercial-use terms | https://ttsmaker.com/ |
| Google AI Studio | Test expressive text-to-speech voices | Access, quotas and terms can change; verify commercial rights for the exact model/output | https://aistudio.google.com/ |

## 5) Three practical ₹0 production recipes

### Recipe A — AI cinematic short
1. Ask the Creative Developer for 4–6 shots, each 4–8 seconds, with one consistent character and one visual reference.
2. Generate a reference image first; reuse it for image-to-video shots where supported.
3. Use free credits across available tools rather than expecting one site to generate every shot for free.
4. Generate narration in TTSMaker or another tool whose terms permit the intended use.
5. Assemble in VN/Clipchamp/CapCut; add manually reviewed captions and platform-cleared music.

### Recipe B — Fast relatable reel (best starting format for Dil Se Scroll)
1. Use 1–3 original images or phone-recorded shots.
2. Add motion using editor zoom/pan, cuts and simple transitions rather than spending video-generation credits on every shot.
3. Use a short voice-over or no voice-over; captions carry the joke/story.
4. Finish in VN or Clipchamp. This is usually more reliable than chasing free AI video credits.

### Recipe C — Faceless explainer for CodersAdda
1. Script a 20–40 second fact-checked tip.
2. Record screen footage, code, slides or browser demo.
3. Add a TTS narration only if it improves clarity.
4. Use captions and simple visual highlights; avoid generic AI stock scenes.

## 6) Free access is not automatically commercial permission

Before using AI video, voice, music or images in public or monetized content, confirm:
- The free plan permits the intended publishing or commercial use.
- Attribution is not required—or add it if required.
- Voice cloning is consensual and does not imitate a public figure or another creator without permission.
- The music is platform-cleared or licensed for the intended use.
- Watermarks and AI disclosure requirements are followed.
- The tool's current terms and platform rules are checked on the day of use.

Never clone a person's voice without their permission. Do not use AI to impersonate someone deceptively.
