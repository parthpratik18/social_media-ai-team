# Zero-budget content production workflow

## Goal
Turn an approved daily idea into a publishable short using tools the user can access for free today, with minimal time and no paid subscription assumed.

## Production order
1. **Idea and script:** Use the Daily AI Content Team output; choose a strong hook, one idea, and a short payoff.
2. **Storyboard:** Generate 4–6 shots with duration, character, framing, movement, environment, caption placement and continuity notes.
3. **Visual generation:** First generate a character/scene reference image if consistency matters. Use free AI video credits for only the most valuable shots. If credits are exhausted, use original photos, phone footage, motion on still images and simple editing.
4. **Voice:** Create a short narration. Try a free TTS option only after checking export and commercial-use conditions. Listen for pronunciation, pacing and Hindi/Hinglish naturalness. Re-record or revise awkward lines.
5. **Edit:** Assemble in VN, Clipchamp, CapCut or another accessible free editor. Use 9:16, readable captions, a clear first-frame hook, quick cuts and a clean ending.
6. **Sound:** Use original audio or platform-cleared/licensed sounds. Do not download trending music and assume it is cleared for every platform.
7. **Quality check:** Watch the final video without sound and with sound. Check captions, spelling, visual artifacts, pacing, voice, rights, and whether the ending encourages a share/save/comment naturally.
8. **Publish manually:** Adapt caption and title for each platform; do not automatically repost copyrighted material.
9. **Measure:** Track views, average watch time, retention, shares, saves, comments and follows. Compare formats over time rather than declaring a winner from one post.

## Prompt template for the Creative Developer

Create a vertical 9:16 video production pack for the approved idea. Keep it feasible with free or limited-credit tools. Include: (a) hook in the first 1–2 seconds, (b) 15–30 second script unless another length is justified, (c) 4–6 separate scenes with 4–8 seconds each, (d) image prompt and image-to-video prompt for each scene, (e) a consistent character/visual reference, (f) camera, lighting, action and motion details, (g) voice-over text, (h) caption text, (i) platform captions/titles/CTA, (j) editing timeline, (k) fallback plan using still images/phone footage if AI video credits are unavailable. Do not place rendered text inside AI video unless requested; add captions in the editor. Avoid scene prompts that require unsupported physics or complex actions.

## Prompt template for the Reviewer

Review the pack for hook strength, originality, retention, shareability, natural Hindi/Hinglish, production feasibility, visual continuity, factual accuracy (when relevant), tool/license risks and platform suitability. Be strict. Give scores out of 100 and list the 3 biggest weaknesses. Verdict must be APPROVE, REVISE or REJECT. If REVISE, provide exact changes for one revision round. Do not approve generic or derivative content just because it is polished.
